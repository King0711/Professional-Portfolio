Meet Samuel

I build the systems, not just the plan.

Honestly? I'm someone who looks at a broken process and can't leave it alone. That instinct built a 98% accurate AI inventory tracker, ClearFlow (a cashflow dashboard that helped small businesses decide 69% faster), and a volunteer community that went from 12 to 46 people just by being deliberate about communication.

These days it means two jobs at once. At Okhma Global I support the Festival Director and CEO across Film Week Africa, the Africa Film Finance Forum and AFFIRM, owning the institutional systems and research behind an October 2026 delivery. My background is project management, Agile and Lean Six Sigma, but I don't lead with the methodology. I lead with the problem. The framework is just how I think clearly enough to solve it. If you're working on something that needs structure, speed, or a bit of AI backbone, I'd genuinely love to hear about it.
