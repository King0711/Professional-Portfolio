About me

Who is Samuel King?

Honestly? I'm someone who looks at a broken process and can't leave it alone. Whether it's an inventory system running at 60% accuracy or a community that hasn't grown in months — I have to figure out what's wrong and build something better. That instinct has led me to some pretty interesting places: a 98% accurate AI inventory tracker, ClearFlow (a cashflow dashboard that helped small businesses make decisions 69% faster), and growing a volunteer community from 12 to 46 people just by being intentional about communication.

My background is project management — Agile, Lean Six Sigma, Waterfall — but I don't really lead with the methodology. I lead with the problem. The framework is just how I think clearly enough to solve it. Right now, that means pairing traditional PM discipline with AI tools: Claude skills, automation pipelines, dashboards that run themselves. It's a weird and exciting combination.

I'm finishing up a Business Admin degree at BYU while building this AI skills ecosystem on the side. If you're working on something that needs structure, speed, or a bit of AI backbone — I'd genuinely love to hear about it.
